2026-2027 Leadership Announcement

New Co-Presidents Stepping Up

We are excited to announce our new executive leadership for the 2026-2027 season! Sambit Kanjilal and Michael Guo are taking the helm as Co-Presidents of UT Table Tennis.

We extend our heartfelt gratitude to outgoing presidents Qing Chai and Emery He for their outstanding leadership, guidance, and contributions to the club over the past years. Qing has officially graduated and joined our alumni, while Emery will continue as an active player on our A-Team!

Official Club Sponsors Announcement

Welcoming Our 2026-2027 Partners

We are thrilled to announce that UT Table Tennis is officially sponsored by five incredible partners for the 2026-2027 season: Austin Table Tennis Club (ATTC), Terry Black's Barbecue, DIPPD, Russell's Bakery, and Racketstop!

Their generous support directly empowers our competitive collegiate athletes by funding travel to NCTTA regional and national tournaments, subsidizing tournament entries, providing professional training equipment, and keeping our team fueled throughout the season. We are immensely grateful for their partnership and dedication to Longhorn table tennis.
